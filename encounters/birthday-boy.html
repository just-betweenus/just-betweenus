Encounter No. 004 · September 2026 · New York

The Birthday Boy

It was supposed to be an early girls' night.
It was supposed to be an early girls' night.

The Girls' Night

We started at Sweet Linda's, doing exactly what girls' night is supposed to look like: sitting around a table, drinking pornstar martinis, and having a genuinely great time. We were about two cocktails in when we started contemplating a second location.

Unfortunately, a good old-fashioned nor'easter was happening outside. It wasn't actually that bad, but it was raining enough to make us question whether leaving our cozy little table was worth it. Eventually, we decided we'd survive and trekked down the block to Tom & Jerry's.

Which brings me to an important clarification: the nor'easter is not, in fact, the name of the storm. I had to explain this to the Birthday Boy later, who is from New Orleans and apparently had no idea that a nor'easter is simply a type of storm.

Anyway.

We got to Tom & Jerry's and were greeted with a line. In the rain. A line in the rain is truly one of life's more humbling experiences. But we'd already made it this far, so we decided to wait it out.

Eventually, we got inside.

And wow.

The men were seasoned. Older. Hot.

We were all immediately very pleased with the decision to leave Sweet Linda's.

We got drinks and stood around for a bit, taking in our surroundings. Then, completely out of nowhere, a guy walked past us and screamed directly in our faces.

My roommate looked at him and went, "Uh, why did you do that?"

He goes, "You guys should come smoke a cig."

We looked at each other. Our drinks were full. We had literally just gotten them. We couldn't exactly bring them outside.

And then, for reasons I still cannot fully explain, I made the executive decision to simply hand my drink to my roommate and follow this man outside.

The Cigarette

Suddenly, it was just me and him. He gave me a cigarette and lit it for me with his matches, which had a naked woman on the box. Naturally.

We started talking, and he asked me a lot of questions about me. A lot. I didn't actually get nearly as much information out of him, but I liked his vibe. He had pretty eyes, scruffy brown hair, and just generally good energy. And then I found out he was born in Japan.

I love me a Wasian man.

We finished our cigarettes and went back inside, and I figured that was probably the end of the interaction. Cute random cigarette encounter. Fun story. Back to the girls.

Except he came back over to us.

"You guys should come meet my friends."

Okay. I liked that energy.

The Birthday Boy

We met his friends and started blending into the group. Meanwhile, my two friends had somehow found themselves talking to two men who were... less than savory. They looked bored, they looked uninterested, and I simply was not going to participate in whatever was happening over there.

So I stood in the mix, minding my own business, until the Birthday Boy came back around. We started talking again, this time about what I was drinking, and somewhere in there I found out he had just turned 31.

It was his birthday.

He was also drinking.

And doing shrooms.

And Molly.

Hello?

That's crazy.

But also, somehow, it was funny.

Eventually my friends escaped their respective questionable men and came back over. The Birthday Boy looked at all of us and decided we needed another cigarette.

Don't mind if I do.

One More Cigarette

We all went outside together, and it was one of those moments where suddenly the whole night had become a little surreal. We'd gone out for girls' night, ended up at Tom & Jerry's in the rain, and now we were standing outside smoking cigarettes with a men we'd met approximately an hour ago.

And honestly? We were having a great time.

We finished up and went back inside, and I started talking to him again. He got my number and then asked me to hang out the next day. Apparently they were all going out for the football game, and I told him I'd forget about that so he better texted me:

2 PM. Reservoir Bar.

And then we kissed. Not a little polite bar kiss, either. A proper make-out. It was short, it was hot, and I liked it.

The Exit

Eventually, he went to the bathroom. I reconvened with my girls, and we were ready to go. At this point, I had a strategy.

I'm 24. He is 31. His friend was married for seven years. There were a lot of seasoned men in that bar, and suddenly I felt approximately twelve years old by comparison.

So I decided to leave while I was still ahead. I wanted to make an impact. I wanted him to wonder. I wanted to keep him asking for more.

So when he came back, I told him I had to go.

"Where?"

"I just gotta go."

A little mysterious.

He kissed me goodbye, and I walked away.

And then, because apparently the night wasn't finished with me yet, he followed me. He spun me around and kissed me again.

Ugh.

So good.

I walked outside, got into my cab, and immediately texted him:

See you tomorrow.

He hearted it.

And that was that.

For the moment.
