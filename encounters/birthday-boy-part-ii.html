Encounter No. 005

The Birthday Boy, Part II

It was supposed to happen the next day.

September 2026 · New York

The Morning After

I woke up the next morning contemplating whether I was actually going to show up or what, exactly, the vibe was supposed to be.

It was already noon. No shower. No breakfast. No plan.

My roommate ordered Chick-fil-A, which seemed like a reasonable enough place to start.

We decided we'd get ready very quickly and head over to Rocco's with some friends to watch the Gator game. I figured I'd text the Birthday Boy later. Maybe.

So we went to Rocco's with a few guy friends and one of my girlfriends, settled in, and watched the game.

About an hour in, I decided to send it.

"Still out?"

And then—

Nothing.

Radio silence.

Ouch.

The Rightttt

I eventually decided to go to Swift Hibernian with my friend, and while we were settling in, she told me I should text him again.

I said, rightttt.

And then, three hours after my original text, he finally answered.

Apparently, he'd been busy that morning with birthday festivities and was now in Midtown.

But he would love to see me.

Eek.

The only problem was that I was already out and very much not going to Midtown.

So somehow, the plans changed.

I wasn't going to the bar.

He was going to come over.

Around 10.

It was about 7.

Okay. Great.

The Preparation

I was excited.

I was also nervous.

This was finally happening, apparently.

I headed home because I figured I should probably eat something before he came over. I ate quickly, put away my dirty laundry, and turned on the little candle warmer light in my room to make everything feel appropriately moody.

I was, in other words, preparing.

He kept checking in every so often, which made me think this was actually happening.

Then came the comment.

He said something along the lines of, well, he snores, so he could just leave after I used him.

Excuse me?

It was a joke. I knew it was a joke.

But there is a difference between implying you're going to stay over, which I am perfectly happy to hear, and making it sound like I have just invited a stranger over for a transactional experience.

So I told him that was awful.

Thankfully, he immediately agreed and took it back, and we laughed about it.

Crisis averted.

The Delay

At 9:30, I told him he could come whenever.

He replied that his friend was puking and he'd be another hour.

Yuck.

At this point, I was very nervous for him to come over, and now the whole thing was just being postponed over and over.

Whatever.

It wasn't like I was waiting up for him.

Then came another text.

He had to take his friend back. Apparently, the friend was staying with him and would not stop puking.

I believed him.

I was also disappointed.

And, honestly, I knew where this was headed.

I just said, Ok.

Then he asked:

"Can we hang out this week?"

I said yea.

I'll believe it when I see it.

The ball was no longer in my court.

So I went to bed.

The Follow

The next morning, I was lying in bed contemplating the entire situation when I saw it.

A new Instagram follow.

The Birthday Boy.

And then, shortly after, a few likes on some of my best photos.

Well.

He was still interested.

Heheh.

We'll see what happens.
