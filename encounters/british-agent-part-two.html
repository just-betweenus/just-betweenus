Encounter No. 003

The British Agent, Part II

Against all odds, Tuesday turned itself around.
August 2026 · New York
The Invitation

After our first date, The British Agent asked me to hang out the very next day.

Respectfully, absolutely not.

That felt like a lot, too fast, and I had things to do. Or at least I told myself I did. So I politely declined and went about my life.

Then, over the weekend, my phone lit up with:

“Meet me at Banzarbar at 8:00 PM on Tuesday.”

And honestly? How could I say no to a man with a plan?

The Worst Tuesday

Unfortunately, Tuesday proceeded to become one of the worst days of my life.

I shattered an entire bottle of perfume in my bag, effectively ruining my wallet and everything else unfortunate enough to be in its vicinity.

I received a nasty email from someone high up at work. It was storming. By the time I got home, I was so exhausted and irritated that cancelling felt like the only reasonable option.

But I powered through.

I got ready, called approximately everyone in my contacts list to talk me down, and somehow made it out the door.

And thank God I did.

The Speakeasy

The British Agent greeted me with a little smooch at the door, which was objectively adorable.

We checked in for our reservation and were then led into what can only be described as a speakeasy inside the wall.

Tiny. Hidden. Very cute.

And our seats?

In the corner.

Very intimate seating.

He had his hand on my thigh for most of the night, we shared a few kisses, and things were definitely getting cozy.

The Highlight

But honestly, the highlight of the entire night was how much we laughed.

Like, actual crying from laughter.

On multiple occasions.

We talked about growing up, our families, and all the usual second-date getting-to-know-you things without accidentally turning the evening into a therapy session.

Somewhere along the way, the subject of a Son of a Baconator and a Doritos Locos Taco came up.

And that was it.

We were done.

Absolutely reeling.
One More Drink

By the end of the night, he wanted to keep going for another drink, either there or somewhere else, but after the Tuesday I had survived, I simply could not.

It was late, I was tired, and frankly, I had already accomplished enough.

So we settled on sharing one more glass of wine.

How intimate.
The One Direction Poster

At some point, he mentioned that he couldn't wait to see my One Direction poster soon.

Which is apparently British Agent code for:

“When am I coming to your apartment?”

We'll see if he ever makes it there. ;)

The Exit

We eventually left, sealed the night with a kiss, and I climbed into my Uber feeling just a little bit on top of the world.

But let's all calm down.

We're not getting too excited yet.
The Verdict
9/10
The universe tried extremely hard to stop this date from happening. Thankfully, I ignored it.
Initial Impression Here we go again.
Status See you next time ;)
