Encounter No. 002

The Southern Belle

A reunion, a questionable outfit, an argument about women and absolutely no sleep.
August 2026 · New York
The History

I have known The Southern Belle since around 2022.

I visited one of his friends at college, we hit it off and spent the weekend together. A couple months later, he came to visit me at school for my twenty first birthday.

Then I quickly cut him loose.

Nothing dramatic. I just get tired of people.

The Return

Somehow, years later, he was back.

He wanted to stay for the entire weekend. I said absolutely not. Dinner and drinks was enough of a reunion for me.

He arrived at my apartment while I was getting ready, already calling me repeatedly because he was confused about something.

Fine. Whatever.

Then I opened the door.

The First Impression
South Carolina collared shirt. Strange boots. Hole in the pants.

I looked at him and immediately thought: we are supposed to be going somewhere nice tonight.

I was dressed. I had a plan. Dinner followed by a cocktail bar.

And this was what he chose.

Unfortunately, the outfit was only the beginning.

The toilet seat was left up. He walked through my apartment with his shoes on. He kept trying to sit on my bed in clothes he had been wearing on the train.

I told him that if he continued, I would send him home.

I meant it.

The Dinner

Before dinner got too far along, I decided to get something out of the way.

I asked him what exactly his intentions were for the night.

Eventually, after some dancing around the question, the answer became clear.

Sex.

I told him that was not happening.

He said fine. I said good.

And honestly, dinner itself was fine. We talked. We caught up. Nothing terrible.

Then we went to the cocktail bar.

The Conversation

Somewhere between martinis and midnight, we started talking about women.

Specifically, what it is like to be a young woman in New York. Being underestimated at work. Being uncomfortable on the subway. Being looked at in ways that make you feel less like a person and more like something being evaluated.

I was trying to explain my experience.

He was trying to explain it back to me.

Every point became a solution. Every experience became something I should have handled differently.

Then he informed me that being a man was hard too.

He also did not see a problem with men looking at women like pieces of meat.

At that point, I was done.

I told him directly that I thought the way he was speaking was disrespectful, especially considering he has sisters and nieces.

He started arguing with me.

Of course he did.

The Sleeping Situation

By the time we got home, I had already decided I wanted absolutely nothing to do with him.

I made the rules very clear.

No touching. No cuddling. No funny business. You are here as a friend.

He agreed.

Then I spent the entire night waking him up because he kept slowly moving further and further into my side of the bed.

I counted.

Eight times.

Eight times I woke him up, told him to move over and tried to reclaim my own bed.

I eventually put a pillow between us like a physical border because apparently my verbal one was not enough.

By morning, I had barely slept.

He asked how I slept.

I told him terribly.

The Exit

He only remembered me waking him up once.

Convenient.

I was ready for him to leave. I was ready to get away from him. I was ready to sleep for approximately twelve hours.

So he left to meet his friends, and I went home for the weekend.

I got on the subway and thought about the entire experience.

And honestly?

It could not have gone worse.

The Verdict
2/10
Points awarded for dinner being fine. Points immediately removed for everything that happened after.
Would I see him again? No.
Should I have left this in 2022? Absolutely.
