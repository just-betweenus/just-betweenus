Encounter No. 001

The British Agent

Met on Tinder. He had an accent. Things escalated.
August 2026 · New York
The Setup

Tinder. Yes, I know.

I showed up fashionably late, which would have been perfectly chic if I had actually entered the correct door. Instead, I walked into the wrong entrance and ended up standing in a lobby while The British Agent was apparently outside the other door, wondering where the hell I was.

Eventually, he came into the lobby to find me. First impression: hot. Tall. Twenty-nine. Older man. Love.

He was dressed a little more casually than I expected, but whatever. I liked my outfit, I looked hot and chic, and that's really what matters.

The First Impression
ACCENT. BRITISH.

Then he opened his mouth. I don't even remember what he said. British. I died inside.

The Date

We got to our table and started talking, and somehow the conversation just kept going. Movies. Harry Potter. One Direction. Being British. Life. Me. Him. Everything.

He was funny, attractive, and genuinely seemed interested in getting to know me, which is always a nice surprise when you meet someone on Tinder.

And somewhere along the way, we started making out. Then we made out again. And again.

I'm not exactly sure when this became the activity of the evening, but I wasn't complaining.

The Classified Information

At some point I asked about his job. He told me he couldn't really say.

Excuse me?

Apparently it's government military. He also has a degree in chemistry.

So naturally my immediate conclusion was:

Is he making bombs?

I have no evidence to support this theory. But I'm keeping it open.

The Hotel Situation

Eventually he mentioned that he was staying at a hotel because he currently lives in the Hamptons. And I immediately thought: Oh, I see where this is going.

He asked if I wanted to come back with him. I told him, essentially, do I look like a girl who goes home with a guy on the first date?

He asked if that was a bad thing.

And I said, well... no. But it's not me.

Then he hit me with:

"I'm a guy. I have to try."

Ew.

So I told him:

You don't really.

He laughed. And thankfully, we moved on.

His hand was on my thigh. There was chemistry. There was temptation. But I held the line.

I am a strong woman.

The Exit

Eventually I told him I had to go. He got the check, obviously, and then waited outside with me for my Uber.

He held my hand. I didn't hate it. We kissed goodbye, I got into my Uber, and that was that.

The Aftermath

Or so I thought.

He messaged me to make sure I got home safely. Then he asked for my number because, until that point, we'd only been talking on Tinder.

The next day?

Voice memos.

Multiple.

And listen — I know I said we need to make the men chase, but hearing a British man send you a voice memo is a very specific kind of test of character.

He also wants to see me again. Immediately.

Which brings us to the current situation: He is hot. He is mysterious. He may or may not make bombs. He sends voice memos. And he wants a second date.

The Verdict
9/10
Points deducted for unclear intentions. Points restored for the accent.
Would I see him again? Yes.
Will I immediately be available? Absolutely not.
