001

Bar Sprezzatura

Italian summer vibes on a Hell's Kitchen rooftop. I went for happy hour and the food was small portions but pretty good. The spritz was good, but the espresso martini was the real winner. Three shared apps and about two and a half drinks each came out to $55 per person before tip, which honestly feels pretty reasonable for a rooftop in Manhattan.

Swan Club

An intimate little cocktail bar on the Lower East Side with moody lighting and not many seats. The martini was REALLY good. I didnt get the tab, so I cant speak to the price, but this feels much more like a date night spot than a girls night spot. For girls night, I think we can find somewhere better. For a date, absolutely.
