003

Miss Nellie's

Old-timey speakeasy vibes in Hell's Kitchen. The American food is good, but nothing out of the ordinary—the atmosphere is definitely the reason to go. I had The Filthy Martini, which unfortunately was neither filthy nor particularly enjoyable. It tasted deathly of vermouth. Skip that one. One tip: when making a reservation, ask for the Textile Room to get seated in the cool hidden back room.

004

The Rosevale Cocktail Room

Jazz bar vibes in Hell's Kitchen with an amazing martini—and as many olives as you could want. I got three, obviously. The inside can get pretty loud, so the outdoor patio is definitely the move. Really chill, good vibes, and a great place to sit and have a drink.
