The Rosevale Cocktail Room

Jazz bar vibes in Hell's Kitchen with an amazing martini—and as many olives as you could want. I got three, obviously. The inside can get pretty loud, so the outdoor patio is definitely the move. Really chill and good vibes.

Drinks · 9 / 10
New York · Dinner

Miss Nellie's

Old-timey speakeasy vibes in Hell's Kitchen. The American food is good, but nothing out of the ordinary—the atmosphere is definitely the reason to go. The Filthy Martini was not filthy and tasted deathly of vermouth. Ask for the Textile Room when making a reservation for the cool back room.

Dinner · 7 / 10

Swan Club

An intimate little cocktail bar on the Lower East Side with moody lighting, limited seating and a very good martini.

Bar Sprezzatura

Rooftop Italian summer vibes in Hell's Kitchen. A very good place to end the workday with a spritz and an espresso martini.
